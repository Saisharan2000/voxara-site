Delete your account and data

Your notes are yours. You can delete your Voxara account, and everything stored with it, at any time — from inside the app or by email. Here is exactly how, and exactly what happens.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Voxara and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon on the home screen).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your account and all of its content are erased immediately.

Delete by email

If you can't sign in, email hello@voxara.store from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Voxara account”.

We verify the request comes from the account owner, then delete the account within 30 days — usually much sooner. We'll confirm by email when it's done.

Delete individual notes (without deleting your account)

You don't have to delete everything to delete something. Any single note can be removed on its own, and your account stays exactly as it is:

  1. Open the note you want to remove.
  2. Tap the … menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Delete note.

The note and anything Voxara derived from it are erased from your account. You can repeat this for as many notes as you like. If you'd rather we removed specific data for you, email hello@voxara.store and tell us what to remove — we'll confirm when it's done.

What is kept, and why

A small record that a consent or deletion request happened is kept in an anonymised form — it is no longer linked to you or to any note, and it exists only so we can show these steps were followed. Nothing of your content remains.

Routine encrypted backups may hold residual data for a short period before they age out on their normal schedule. That data is not accessible in the app and is not restored to your account.

Deleting your account is permanent. There is no recovery, and support cannot restore deleted notes — including for us. If you want to keep your notes, export them first from Settings → What's stored, where you can download everything as a file.
