Privacy

Privacy is not a feature.
It's the architecture.

Most voice apps upload your audio to the cloud. Voxara processes everything on your device by default. When we say private, we built it that way.

On-device transcription

Audio stays on your phone. Whisper AI runs locally — no internet connection needed for transcription.

User-held encryption keys

AES-256-GCM encryption. Your key, your passphrase. We never see your content — even if our servers were compromised.

No model training

Your voice data is never used to train AI models. This is guaranteed by our API provider agreements.

Hard deletion

Delete means delete. Account removal permanently erases all data, keys, and embeddings. Nothing is soft-deleted.
